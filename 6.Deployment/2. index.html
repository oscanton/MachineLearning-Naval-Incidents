Vessel Incident Prediction System

Welcome to the Vessel Incident Prediction System. Following tools use Random Forest models to forecast both the probability and type of vessel incidents.

Data Sources: This project's models were trained on naval incident data from the US Coast Guard (USCG) covering the years 2002 to 2015, alongside meteorological data from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).

Development process summary:

  • Data Mining: Gathered data from USCG and NOAA public websites.
  • Data Cleaning and Preprocessing: Ensured accuracy and completeness of the data.
  • Exploratory Data Analysis: Conducted EDA to understand the data and select the appropriate models.
  • Model Training and Comparison: Trained various machine learning models and compared their performance. Random Forest was chosen for its robust performance and ease of interpretation.
  • Deployment: Used FastAPI and Unicorn for a local example, providing efficient data handling and reliable performance. Created forms for single and multiple boat data entry to facilitate predictions.

Explore the forms below to see how the system predicts incident involvement and incident type probability:
